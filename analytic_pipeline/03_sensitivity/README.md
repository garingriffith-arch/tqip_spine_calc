# Prespecified and targeted robustness analyses

The frozen local study evaluated clinical and contextual subgroup performance; missingness strata; airway-state sensitivity; broad-phenotype transport; exclusion of early deaths; death-or-prolonged-resource composites; concurrent cervical/TBI and thoracic/lumbar injury phenotypes; and year/era performance.

No routine transfer-in sensitivity was undertaken: transfer-in patients have a different prediction-time baseline because their receiving-centre trajectory begins after pre-transfer treatment/stabilization. Subsequent transfer-outs do not have a complete observed index-centre trajectory.

Facility-level heterogeneity was not assessed because usable facility identifiers were unavailable.

**Archive status:** Final local analysis scripts and aggregate outputs have not yet been transferred here. The public archive does not claim to include all subgroup model predictions or patient-level data.
