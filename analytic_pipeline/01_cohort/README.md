# Cohort construction and endpoint definitions

This stage will archive the final ACS TQP PUF 2020–2024 harmonization/phenotype workflow, including:

- Age 18–89 and qualifying narrow acute thoracolumbar fracture, cord, or root/cauda phenotype;
- direct-presentation restriction; transfer-in, unknown transfer status, subsequent transfer-out, AMA, and non-observable downstream trajectory exclusions;
- diagnosis-field reconstruction and mandatory flag/concordance checks;
- curated thoracolumbar therapeutic ICD-10-PCS operative-treatment endpoint and nonmutually exclusive categories.

**Frozen cohort:** N=424,017; admission-year counts: 2020 75,237; 2021 83,076; 2022 85,301; 2023 89,754; 2024 90,649.

**Archive status:** The final runnable cohort/phenotype sources and non-patient-level operation code map are not yet included here. The root legacy app's data/ files are not the manuscript cohort.

No patient-level PUFs, extracts, IDs, or derived modeling datasets may be published.
